How one sound is made

Each voice follows the same path a sample took through the PlayStation's sound chip. Nothing is stored: every number below is worked out from the sound's name and the world seed.

  1. Hash the nameWorld seed, then category, sound, voice and parameter slot, each hashed from the one above.
  2. Write a source sampleA short FM tone, wavetable, noise loop or pitch-drop sine, computed sample by sample.
  3. Squeeze it through ADPCM28 samples become 16 bytes, then get decoded again, which adds the console's grain.
  4. Play it as a voiceA pitch counter reads the sample through 4-point Gaussian smoothing, shaped by an ADSR envelope.
  5. Mix and add reverbTwo or three voices are panned, summed and sent through a half-rate reverb.

Source and documentation: github.com/MushroomFleet/zerosounds2-JSX
